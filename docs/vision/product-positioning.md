# Product Positioning

## What is Knowledge Map AI?

A relationship-first visual knowledge map that surfaces hidden connections across a local document vault.

## Positioning Statement

For knowledge workers who are drowning in disconnected notes and documents, Knowledge Map AI is a local-first visual explorer that reveals the relationships between ideas — so you can think in networks, not folders.

## What makes it different

- **Relationship-first** — the graph is the primary UI, not a secondary view bolted onto a file list
- **Local-first** — your data never leaves your machine; no cloud account required
- **Demo-ready** — ships with curated domain vaults (accounting, exhibition) to show the concept without onboarding friction
- **Inspired by Rowboat** — clean, purposeful UI; no visual noise

## What it is NOT

- Not a SaaS product
- Not a note-taking app (it reads vaults you already have)
- Not a search engine (it surfaces relationships, not keyword matches)
- Not an AI chatbot (the map is the interface)

## Target demo audience

- Operators evaluating AI knowledge tools for their team
- Founders wanting to show the "connected knowledge" concept to investors
- Domain experts (accountants, curators) who need to navigate dense relationship networks

## Status

MVP — mock data, no backend, no AI API. Focus is on the visual interaction model.
